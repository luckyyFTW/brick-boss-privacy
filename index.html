Brick Boss – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Brick Boss is a game made by Potato Studio (an independent developer). This page explains what happens with your information when you play. Short version: we don't collect anything about you ourselves. The only outside service in the game is Google AdMob, which shows ads.

What we collect

Nothing. Brick Boss has no sign-up, no accounts and no analytics. We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we don't run our own servers.

Your saved game

Your progress (money, buildings, settings and so on) is saved only on your phone. It never leaves your device and we can't see it. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress is deleted.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Some ads you choose to watch for rewards; others show now and then between play. We have set the ads up to be safe for all ages:

  • Ads are not personalised: they are not based on your interests or what you do in other apps.
  • Ad requests are marked as coming from an app that may be used by children, so Google treats them under its rules for children.
  • Ad content is limited to the "General audience" rating.
  • The game does not use your device's advertising ID.

To show and count ads, Google may still process some technical information, such as your IP address, device type and app version. Google handles this under its own policy: How Google uses information in ads and the Google Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU or UK), the game asks for your consent before showing ads.

Sharing photos

The game has a photo button. If you tap Share, your phone's own share menu opens and you choose where the picture goes. We never see or keep these photos.

Links

The game has buttons that open our YouTube and Instagram pages, the Google Play page to rate the game, and your email app to contact us. Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Brick Boss can be enjoyed by players of all ages. We don't knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child's information has reached us somehow, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
